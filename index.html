about

I'm a Machine Learning Researcher at the Choroidal Analysis and Research (CAR) Lab at the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine, working under Dr. Jay Chhablani (Professor of Ophthalmology & NIH-funded retina specialist) and Dr. Kiran Kumar Vupparaboina (Research Assistant Professor, AI & medical imaging).

My work focuses on retinal OCT imaging for age-related macular degeneration (AMD) — spanning self-supervised representation learning, multi-label biomarker classification, segmentation pipelines, and multimodal image registration. I work closely with clinicians to build AI tools that are actually useful at the bedside.

Before Pitt, I completed my B.Tech in Electrical Engineering at IIT Hyderabad (CGPA 8.17/10), where I first got pulled into computer vision. I've also interned at Philips Innovation Campus, Unstudio.ai, FrinksAI, and Snazzy (YC-backed) along the way.
