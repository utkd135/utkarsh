about

I'm a PhD student at the University of Pittsburgh working on medical image analysis — specifically OCT retinal imaging for age-related macular degeneration (AMD). My research spans self-supervised learning, biomarker classification, segmentation pipelines, and multi-modal image registration.

Before Pitt, I did my undergraduate in Electrical Engineering at IIT Hyderabad, where I first got pulled into computer vision and learned that nothing beats a well-designed loss function.

research

  • LeJEPA: domain-adapted SSL for retinal OCT

    Designing augmentation strategies for a joint-embedding predictive architecture applied to Spectralis OCT B-scans. Targeting AMD biomarker classification (drusen, PED, hypertransmission) with 5-fold patient-level cross-validation. Best config reaches 85% fine-tune macro AUROC, outperforming ImageNet baselines by ~11 AUROC points.

    Self-supervised learning OCT imaging AMD IEEE TMI (under review)
  • RETFound fine-tuning for multi-label biomarker detection

    Adapting RETFound (ViT-Large masked autoencoder pretrained on 1.6M retinal images) via linear probing and full fine-tuning for multi-label AMD biomarker classification on OCT B-scans.

    ViT Foundation models Retinal imaging
  • Geographic atrophy quantification pipeline

    BatchGAPipeline for GA area quantification from en-face OCT — processing ~4,000 volumes with histogram matching, nnU-Net segmentation, and producer-consumer threading.

    Segmentation nnU-Net Geographic atrophy
