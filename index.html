AAbout

I work at the CAR Lab under Dr. Jay Chhablani (Professor of Ophthalmology & retina specialist), Dr. Kiran Kumar Vupparaboina (AI & medical imaging), and Dr. Sandeep Bollepalli.

My focus is retinal OCT for age-related macular degeneration — self-supervised representation learning, multi-label biomarker classification, segmentation pipelines, and multimodal image registration. I sit close to clinicians, and I care about models that survive contact with a real clinic: different devices, messy scans, rare biomarkers.

Before Pitt: B.Tech in Electrical Engineering at IIT Hyderabad, where computer vision first pulled me in — plus internships at Philips Innovation Campus, Unstudio.ai, FrinksAI, and Snazzy (YC-backed).
